### INGREDIENTS :

###### 1 cup Kodo Millet powder/flour

###### 1/2 cup Wheat flour

###### 1/2 cup Jowar flour

###### 1 table spoon saunf/Fennel seeds

###### Salt to taste

###### 2 table spoons cooking oil (While making Dough)

### METHOD :

###### Mix all above ingredients like typical Chapathi (Indian flat roti) dough and keep it covered with a wet cloth for 30 minutes.

###### Roll the multi layered flat pooris with rolling pin and make Pooris by frying in heated cooking oil.

###### NOTE: Puri can be made with little millet, barnyard millet and browntop milllet too.
