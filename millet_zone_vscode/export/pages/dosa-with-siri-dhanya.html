DOSA with millet zone

'SIRI DHANYA' MILLETs

INGREDIENTS:

• 4 cups of Kodo Millet

• 1 cup of Poha (Flattened Rice)

• 3/4 cup of Black Gram (Udad dal- split & dehusked)

• 1 table spoon of Methi/Fenugreek seeds

• Curd/Home made Yoghurt to soak ingredients.

METHOD:

• ALL SHOULD BE SOAKED IN CURD/Home Made Yoghurt for 9-10 hours

• Grind all the above in grinder in to a fine paste. Add salt according to taste.

• Ferment this paste for 10 hours.

• Dosa Mix is Ready. Make your dosa and serve with chutney.

SET DOSA with

KODO MILLET/Arikalu
