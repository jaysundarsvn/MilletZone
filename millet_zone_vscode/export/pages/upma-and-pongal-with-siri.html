'SIRI DHANYA' MILLET

INGREDIENTS:

• 1 cup of Foxtail Millets

• 3⁄4 th cup of Greengram/Mung Half split with Greencover

• 1 table spoon Oil/Ghee

• 1 tb Jeera/Cumin

• 1 tb Black pepper Powder

• 2 Green chillies

• Curry Leaves 15

METHOD:

• Lightly fry both the grains separately in a kadai for 3-4 minutes to dehydrate them.

• Cook them separately in cooker at 1:2 ratio with water.

• After they are cooked, mix them together, add needed Salt, add TADKA and Mix well.

• Again keep it on Low flame on stove, add 1 or 2 spoons of water if needed and mix well.

• Pongal is ready !!
