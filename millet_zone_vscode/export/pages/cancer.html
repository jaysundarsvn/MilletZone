Cancer protocols have been mainly divided into 2 parts: (Refer Charts Below)

DECOCTIONS/KASHAYA and MILLET SIRIDHANYA .

• DECOCTION/KASHAYA :

Make the leaves/ roots/ plant parts into chopped, small parts (6 leaves if the leaves are big and fist full of leaves if leaves are small).

The chopped parts and leaves are to be added to 1.5 -2 cups of water. Boil on medium heat for about 4 minutes, allowing it to be infused into the water. Filter the contents. Consume it when luke-warm.

Ideally, the kashayas should be consumed without any added jaggery. But one may add few drops of liquid PURE PALM JAGGERY for taste. Do not add sugar.

The best time to consume the KASHAYA is early MORNING, ON EMPTY STOMACH. Also one hour before lunch and dinner.

Cancer patients have specific decoctions in the afternoon/evening.

(a total of 3 times a day- Morning after wake up, again at 4 pm, again 1 hr before dinner. )

Leaves of Each tree must be repeated for one week.

• MILLET SIRIDHANYA

The millets must be used as a staple diet for BREAKFAST, LUNCH and DINNER.

Same grain must be used for breakfast, lunch and dinner. The grains must not be mixed.

The grain must be soaked for 5-6 hours prior to cooking. Check the ways to prepare food with millets in the 'RECIPE' page.

AMBALI or "fermented MASHED Millet BROTH' & ITS IMPORTANCE

Dr Khadar always suggests for great results, using recommended Siridhanya as 'Ambali' or 'Fermented broth' for first 10 weeks if you are seriously fighting any disease . ( Importantly do not throw away any solid/liquid part of it )

Pre-soak Siridhanya/broken/flour for 5 to 7 hours using 1: 7 water (cook in same soaked water) and cook more than usual, mash it thoroughly, Keep a lid of porous cloth on it for 12 hours and consume after 12 hours with all vegetables and curries, Dal & little pickle, buttermilk etc- as you wish.

For new consumers, a point to remember : Siridhanya like Kodo millet and Browntop millet let out some color in to the soaked water. Do not be apprehensive thinking that it is artificially 'added' color. No, it is natural. Kodo Millet gives out Brick Red color and Browntop millet gives our green tinge to the water. So, wish you all the health from Unpolished Siridhanya.
